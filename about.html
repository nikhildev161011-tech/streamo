About Videoz

Namaste! This website Videoz has been created purely as a web development practice project. Its main objective is to practice HTML, CSS, and responsive layouts such as Grid and Flexbox.

All the video highlights and content displayed on this platform are integrated via the official <iframe> embed feature provided by third-party platforms like YouTube. We do not host or store any video files on our own servers.

2. Copyright & Third-Party Rights

All video copyrights, trademarks, and intellectual property rights belong strictly to their original creators and respective YouTube channels. This website does not intend to generate any commercial profit or commit copyright infringement.

3. Content Removal Request (DMCA Notice)

If any copyright holder or creator has any objections to their content appearing on this website, they can contact us immediately. As soon as we receive the notification, we will remove that video or link from our practice website without any delay.
